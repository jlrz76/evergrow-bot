## 🌟 Why Use Evergrow Bot?

In Evergrow, crops decay if not harvested promptly, losing up to **50% of their yield**. Keeping your browser or phone screen active 24/7 is exhausting. 

**Evergrow Bot runs in the cloud on dedicated high-speed servers to manage your farm around the clock.**

### Key Features
- ⚡ **Instant Auto-Harvest**: Senses crop maturity immediately and triggers harvests with sub-second latency for **100% maximum fresh yield** (0% decay penalty).
- 🌻 **Smart Auto-Replant & Auto-Buy**: Detects empty plots and automatically purchases Sunbud seeds and replants them as soon as funds or plots become available.
- ⏱️ **Real-Time Farm Status**: Send `/status` or `/plants` in Telegram anytime to check exact remaining countdowns, active stages (e.g. Stage 3/4), and GROW/Petals balances.
- 🛡️ **Session Health & Malfunction Guard**: Automatically alerts you via Telegram if your game session expires or encounters issues so your farm never stops earning.
- ☁️ **100% Cloud-Hosted**: No need to keep your PC on, install Python, or drain your phone battery. Everything runs automatically 24/7 in the cloud.

## 🚀 How to Get Started (Takes 30 Seconds)

No installation or coding required! Follow these 3 simple steps:

### 1. Open the Bot
Click here: **[@EvergrowAlert_Bot](https://t.me/EvergrowAlert_Bot)** on Telegram and press **Start** (`/start`).  
*(Every new user automatically receives a **24-Hour Free Trial**!)*

### 2. Connect Your Game Session
In the Telegram bot, send:
```text
/login <your_serverToken>
```

> 💡 **Don't know where to find your `serverToken`?**  
> Type `/guide` in the bot for a fast 10-second guide:
> 1. Open [i.playevergrow.com](https://i.playevergrow.com/) on PC.
> 2. Press **F12** ➔ Click **Console**.
> 3. Paste: `copy(JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('evergrow:session:v1')).serverToken)`
> 4. Paste the copied token back into [@EvergrowAlert_Bot](https://t.me/EvergrowAlert_Bot) with `/login <token>`.

### 3. Sit Back & Watch Your Farm Grow!
Type `/status` to verify your connected farm. Your crops will now be harvested and replanted automatically 24 hours a day, 7 days a week!
